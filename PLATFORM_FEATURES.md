# Botswana National Museum — Platform Features & Capabilities

**Platform:** Botswana National Museum mobile web app
**Last updated:** September 2026

---

## 1. Overview

The Botswana National Museum platform is a mobile-first web application that brings the museum and the culture of Botswana to visitors' phones and browsers. It combines a rich digital museum experience — exhibitions, artifacts, history, culture, and dance — with practical tools such as ticket reservations, restaurant bookings, venue rentals, event listings, and a merchandise catalog.

The platform is built as a self-contained web app. Its content is stored directly within the app, which means it works instantly even when opened from a device's file storage or served without a database, and it renders cleanly in any modern browser. It is hosted publicly via GitHub Pages and updated through the project's Git repository.

The experience is organized around five main sections accessed through a tab bar at the bottom of the screen:

| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **About** | The museum itself — history, exhibits, opening times, contacts, and other museums in Botswana |
| **Engage** | Things to do — bookings, events, on-display items, and the merchandise shop |
| **Explore** | Browse content by category — artifacts, wildlife, plants, foods, totems, and more |
| **Learn** | Educational content — cultures, traditional dances, "Around Botswana" guides, and trivia games |
| **Menu** | Booking summary, shop, and contact/feedback options |

Each tab opens a home screen with further drill-down pages, and a back button lets visitors retrace their steps. The navigation bar and logo automatically hide while the visitor scrolls down and reappear when scrolling up, keeping the screen clean and immersive.

## 2. Content & Exhibitions (About / Explore / Learn)

### In-depth cultural articles
The heart of the platform is a collection of **49 long-form, richly written articles** covering museum exhibits, traditional objects, and cultural topics. Each article explains what the item is, how it was made, and its cultural and historical meaning. Content is written for a general audience and naturally weaves in Setswana terminology (with explanations), giving visitors a genuine window into Botswana's heritage.

Representative topics include:

- **Traditional objects & artifacts** — the wooden stool, the mokoro (dugout canoe), the donker cart (koloi ya tonki), fishing nets and scoops, the snare (seretlho), the big drum (moropa o mogolo), woven baskets (seroto, ditlana), leather sandals, the leopard-skin chair, the dishukeka wig, the quiver bag, tortoise-shell containers, the milk gourd (lekuka), and more.
- **Historic vehicles & symbols** — the Daimler Presidential Car.
- **Heritage clothing & adornment** — German-print shawls, the Herero headdress and dress, Borankana dance attire, waist beads, and copper bangles.
- **Traditional dances** — Borra-borre (Borankana), Hosanna Chokwe, Phathisi, and Setapa, each with its own illustrated page.

### "On Display" photo galleries
A dedicated gallery shows **35 artifacts on display**, each with 2–4 photographs. Visitors can open any artifact to view its full photo set, read its accompanying article, and — in a distinctive visitor-friendly touch — **add their own photo** to an artifact's card right from their device (the photo is stored in the current session only and not uploaded to any server).

### Museum directory
The About tab profiles the museum itself and lists **17 museums across Botswana** (including Tsodilo Hills, Khama III Memorial, Supa Ngwao, Phuthadikobo, Francistown City, Kgosi Bathoen II, and many more). Each museum page includes its own descriptive article, photographs, a gallery, and — for the main museum — opening times and contact details. Featured photo galleries let visitors scroll through museum imagery.

### Browse by category (Explore)
The Explore tab organizes content into browsable categories so visitors can dig into topics of interest:

- **Presidential Legacy** — profiles of Botswana's presidents.
- **Naming & Origins** — Setswana naming conventions and origins.
- **Gaborone Trails** — walking guides to the capital.
- **Monuments & Sacred Sites** — cultural landmarks.
- **Crafts & Artisanship** — traditional skilled crafts.
- **Flora & Fauna** — the natural world of Botswana.
- **Kgotla & Botho** — community values and governance.
- **Vernacular Architecture** — traditional building styles.
- **Data-driven collections** — wildlife, birds, plants & trees, foods & beverages, and clan totems, each populated from the museum's structured content.

### Ethnic groups & cultures (Learn)
Educational pages cover **ethnic groups and cultures**, with sub-topics on traditions, ceremonies, music, dance, folklore, proverbs, languages, and kingdoms.

### "Around Botswana" (Learn)
A mini travel guide with **9 landmark tiles and 10 full articles** — the Baobab "Prison Tree", kgotla meeting places, woodcarving, baskets (mongongo / mokola basketry), the mokoro, mongongo nuts, phane worms, and Tsodilo Hills.

## 3. Booking & Reservations (Engage)

The Engage tab gives visitors quick access to four booking/reservation capabilities:

### 1. Museum entry tickets
- **Five ticket types** with pricing: Standard (P75), Student (P40), Child (P35), Senior (P50), and Group (P60 per person).
- **Add-on options**: a guided tour (+P120) and an audio guide (+P40).
- **Interactive calendar** to pick a date (with past dates blocked), plus time slots throughout the day.
- **Visitor count selector** (up to 20).
- A combined **cost calculator across all bookings that includes 14% tax**.

### 2. Restaurant reservation
- Choose a **date and time** with live availability messaging.
- **Party size selector** (up to 30).
- **Seating preference** (indoor, outdoor, no preference).
- **Special occasions** (birthday, anniversary) and **dietary requirements** (vegan, gluten-free, halal).
- **Pre-order your meal** — a full menu of **15+ dishes across 5 categories** (breakfast/morning, Setswana, modern, desserts, drinks) with prices (P20–P120) and a live running summary of the pre-order.
- Free-text **special requests** box.

### 3. Venue rental
- **Four venue options** with pricing and capacities: Main Hall (capacity 300), Courtyard Pavilion (capacity 150), Seminar Rooms 1–3 (capacity 50–80 each), and presence of the Gallery.
- Select the **event date, duration, and expected guests**.
- Optional **museum café catering**, with catering styles per head (Traditional Setswana, Modern Café Mix, Premium, or build your own).
- **Contact details and special request** fields; inquiries are submitted to the team.

### 4. Summary & checkout
- A **single summary screen** pulls together tickets, restaurant, and venue selections with a **live cost calculator including 14% tax**.
- Visitors enter their **name, email, and phone** to complete all bookings at once.

### Events
A **featured events list** (drawn from a dataset of 37 museum events) links to event detail pages, where visitors can start relevant bookings (private events, groups, schools, meals, or venue-based).

## 5. Learn & Play (Learn)

### Trivia games
Three timed trivia games bring the learning to life:

- **Khama & Kings** — test your knowledge of Botswana's leaders and royal history.
- **Sacred Lands** — questions about sacred sites and landscapes.
- **Culture Quest** — a general culture and heritage challenge.

Each game presents questions with a **5-second countdown timer bar** per question, a live score, a progress bar, instant right/wrong feedback with highlighting, and a graded result page with encouraging messages (e.g., "Curator-level knowledge!"). Players can immediately play again.

### Educational reading
All of the learning topics above — cultures, ceremonies, dances, folklore, languages, kingdoms, and Around Botswana guides — are also readable content within the Learn tab.

## 7. Platform Capabilities

What the platform genuinely does today:

- **Fully self-contained** — all content and data live inside the app itself. It needs no database, no server-side API, and no internet connection to function, and it opens directly from file storage or any static host.
- **Mobile-first design** — designed for phones (optimized for ≤480px screens) with a clean, museum-branded visual style (ocean blue and terracotta palette), and a fixed bottom tab bar for one-thumb navigation.
- **Responsive and instant** — renders and navigates without page reloads.
- **Rich multimedia** — dozens of photographs across galleries, exhibits, museums, dances, and the shop.
- **Visitor photo upload** — visitors can personalize exhibit cards with their own photos during a session.
- **Bilingual flavor** — content is English with authentic Setswana vocabulary and proverbs woven through every topic.
- **Timed, scored quizzes** — real interactivity that rewards learning.
- **Cross-bookable experiences** — tickets, dining, and venue hire all in one visitor flow.

## 8. Current Limitations (for stakeholder awareness)

The following capabilities are the next stage of the platform's roadmap. They are represented in the interface today but are not yet fully live:

- **Booking confirmations are front-end only.** Completing a booking shows a success message and summary, but does not yet take payment, issue tickets, or send confirmation emails. Availability checks are simulated.
- **Add-ons are concept-level.** The guided-tour and audio-guide add-ons appear in the ticket interface but are not yet included in pricing or functionality (a full audio guide player is planned).
- **Merchandise is browse-only.** There is no cart, checkout, or online payment for shop items.
- **Visitor photo uploads are session-only.** Uploaded photos are stored in the current session and disappear on refresh; they are not uploaded anywhere.
- **Single language.** The interface is English; there is no built-in Setswana language toggle yet (though Setswana content features heavily in the material).
- **No push notifications or true offline caching.** While the app runs without a server, it does not yet have install-app (PWA) offline caching or notification capabilities.
